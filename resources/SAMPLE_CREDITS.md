# Twinbox factory library: credits and licenses

Most of the factory samples are synthesized by Twinbox (`tools/gen_samples.py`). These include all kicks, snares/claps and hats, the synth basses and chords of Tie, the TR-808 / TR-909 tom and conga models, and the dissonant hits of Pod.

Some Tie and Pod samples come from free libraries with permissive licenses. They are trimmed, softened with a low-pass filter, and (Tie) tuned to C or combined into C-minor chords:
