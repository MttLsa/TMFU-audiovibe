GLOBAL CONTROL

Central control of the parameters of your original plug-ins in Ableton Live: 8 macro knobs that move the same parameter on many instances at once (Absolute or Relative), without moving, replacing or wrapping the devices.

This installer copies:

  • GLOBAL CONTROL.app into /Applications (universal: Apple Silicon and Intel; it does not process audio);
  • GLOBAL_CONTROL_BRIDGE.amxd, the Max for Live device, into your Ableton User Library (Presets › MIDI Effects › Max MIDI Effect) if the folder exists. Existing files are never overwritten.

Requirements: macOS 11 or later, Ableton Live with Max for Live (Live Suite, or Standard + Max for Live).
