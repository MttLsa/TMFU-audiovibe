# DUALBOX factory library: credits and licenses

Most of the factory samples are synthesized by DUALBOX itself (`tools/gen_samples.py`): all kicks, snares / claps and
hats, the synth basses of Belt, the "Analog" and "Classic" toms and congas of Capsule (synthesis models of classic
analog drum machines), the dissonant hits of Capsule and all CLICK transients.

Some Belt and Capsule samples come from free libraries with permissive licenses. They are trimmed, softened with a
low-pass filter and (Belt) tuned to C or combined into C-minor chords:
